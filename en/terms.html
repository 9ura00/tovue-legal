What ToVue is

ToVue is a virtual sightseeing app. You move a virtual camera over Apple Maps (MapKit) imagery and 3D buildings. It does not connect to or control any real drone or aircraft, and it is not a survey, navigation, aviation or safety tool. Positions, altitudes and place names are approximations derived from map data and must not be relied on for any real-world decision.

Map content and network

Map imagery, 3D buildings, place search and place names are provided by Apple and may be incomplete, outdated or unavailable in some regions. An internet connection is required. Your carrier may charge for mobile data, and faster flight tends to load more map data.

Photos you create

Photos are rendered on your device from the map view only, without controls or ads. They always keep Apple's map attribution. Unless you have bought Remove Ads & Watermark, they also show a small ToVue watermark. You choose whether the place name, coordinates and altitude are added. If you share photos, you are responsible for doing so lawfully and in line with the map provider's attribution and usage terms. Do not remove or hide the map attribution.

Advertising

The free version may show a banner advertisement from Google AdMob in a separate strip above the map. After you move to a new place with search or the map picker, it may also show a full-screen advertisement at most once every few minutes. Full-screen ads are never shown while you are flying with the controls, and you can close them. Ads never appear in photos. Advertising is subject to the consent choices described in the Privacy Policy.

Remove Ads & Watermark purchase

Remove Ads & Watermark is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple. It is not a subscription. Once the purchase is verified, it removes all advertisements (banner and full-screen) and the ToVue watermark from photos taken afterwards. Apple Maps attribution is never removed. The App Store shows the price before you buy. Use Restore Purchases on a device signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, ads and the watermark return.
