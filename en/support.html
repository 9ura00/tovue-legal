Does ToVue use my location?

No. ToVue never uses GPS and never asks for location permission. The starting place follows your device's Region setting.

The map or 3D buildings don't appear.

An internet connection is required. 3D buildings and imagery come from Apple Maps and vary by area; some places are shown in less detail.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the menu (top right) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I fly with one hand?

Menu > Controls > One-Hand Mode. Touch the stick to fly forward, push up or down to climb or descend, and left or right to turn.

How do I delete my data?

ToVue keeps only settings on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Photos you saved stay in your photo library.
