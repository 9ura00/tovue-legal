Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-28. This policy describes data handling by ToVue and the external services used by the app. Contact: tovue00@gmail.com.

No location access, no account, no developer server

The app does not use GPS and never asks for location permission. The first view is chosen from your device's Region setting only. There is no sign-in or account, the developer does not operate a server that receives data from the app, and the app contains no analytics SDK.

Apple Maps

To show the map, search for places and look up the place name of the virtual camera position, the app uses Apple's MapKit and geocoding services. The map area being viewed, your search terms and the virtual camera coordinates are sent to Apple. These are virtual positions you choose, not your real location. Apple's privacy policy applies to this processing.

Google AdMob and consent (UMP)

If you have not bought Remove Ads & ToVue Logo, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform. Where required, for example in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent form is shown, and you can change your choice later with Ad Privacy Choices in the menu. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and requests non-personalized ads. Even non-personalized ads involve Google processing data such as device and app information, IP address and ad interaction data for delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. Google's policies apply; see the links below. The app never passes your virtual flight position, place names or search terms to the ad SDK.

When no ads are requested

The app does not start the advertising or consent SDKs, and requests no ads, while the purchase status is still being checked or when a verified Remove Ads & ToVue Logo purchase is found.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are processed by Apple. The app only checks Apple-signed transaction records on your device to decide whether ads and the ToVue logo are removed. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Photos

Photos are rendered on your device and exist only in the preview until you choose to save or share them with the system share sheet. The app does not upload them. Saving to your photo library requires your permission. A photo contains the virtual place name, coordinates and altitude only when that option is on; it never contains your real location. People who see a shared photo may be able to tell which place was viewed.

Your choices

You can change ad consent in the menu, stop ads with the purchase, and delete the app to remove its local data. Questions about data processed by Apple or Google can be sent to them directly. Other privacy questions: tovue00@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments through Gmail to respond and resolve the issue. Third-party data is handled under each provider's policy.

Apple, Google and GitHub may process data outside your country and apply their own retention rules and safeguards; their policies are linked below. Deleting the app does not delete exported photos, device backups or records retained by these providers.
